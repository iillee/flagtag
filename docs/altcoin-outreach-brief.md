# Flag Tag — Altcoin Community Outreach Brief

## What Is Flag Tag?
Flag Tag is a live multiplayer keep-away game running 24/7 in Decentraland — a browser-based 3D metaverse. Players compete in 5-minute rounds to hold a flag the longest. The game runs on an authoritative server (anti-cheat), tracks scores, and announces winners automatically. It's fully built and live right now.

## The Opportunity
We're launching **live play-to-earn streaming events on X (Twitter)** where we feature a different altcoin community's token as the round prize each week. A partnered YouTube streamer simultaneously broadcasts the event.

### How It Works
1. We announce the event on X, tagging the featured token's community
2. The featured project promotes the event to their audience
3. Players join the Decentraland game through the Decentraland client
4. We stream the gameplay live on X for ~1 hour (roughly 12 rounds)
5. Each round winner receives the featured token as a prize (e.g., 25-50 tokens per win depending on value)
6. Winners are tracked server-side with wallet addresses — distribution happens via an ERC-20 tool after the event

### What the Token Community Gets
- Live gameplay content on X featuring their token as the prize
- Exposure to the Web3 gaming and Decentraland audience
- Real utility/use case for their token (play-to-earn prizes)
- Cross-promotion from our X account and the YouTube streamer
- Clips and highlights they can repost

### What We Need From Them
- Co-promotion of the event to their community (tweet, Discord announcement)
- Optionally: donate tokens for the prize pool (or we purchase a small amount)
- Their community logo/branding for in-game display during the event

## Ideal Partner Profile
We're looking for altcoin communities that meet these criteria:

- **Active community**: Still has real engagement on X and/or Discord (not dead/abandoned)
- **ERC-20 on Ethereum, Polygon, or Base**: We need to be able to distribute tokens easily
- **Small to mid-cap**: Big enough to have a real community, small enough to care about grassroots marketing and novel exposure channels
- **Gaming-friendly or metaverse-adjacent**: Communities open to Web3 gaming integrations (but not required — any active community works)
- **Legitimate project**: Has a working product, team, or roadmap — not obvious pump-and-dumps or memecoins with no community
- **Community size sweet spot**: ~1K-50K followers on X, active Discord with real conversation

## The Ask for Grok
Please generate a list of 20-30 altcoin communities that fit the profile above and are likely to be receptive to this kind of partnership. For each, provide:

1. **Token name and ticker**
2. **Chain** (Ethereum, Polygon, Base, etc.)
3. **X/Twitter handle**
4. **Approximate community size** (X followers, Discord members if known)
5. **Why they'd be a good fit** (e.g., gaming focus, active community, looking for utility, etc.)
6. **Community vibe/receptiveness** — would they likely be open to a fun, low-stakes play-to-earn event?

Prioritize communities that are:
- Actively posting and engaging in the last 30 days
- Known for being community-driven rather than purely speculative
- Open to creative partnerships and grassroots marketing

Avoid:
- Top 50 market cap coins (too big to care)
- Dead projects with no recent activity
- Pure memecoins with no community infrastructure
- Anything with major controversy or regulatory issues
