# Flag Tag — MANA Rewards & Cape System Design

## Overview
Introduce a MANA reward system for Flag Tag, gated behind an exclusive cape wearable to prevent bot abuse and reward dedicated players.

## Cape Wearable
- **Commissioned wearable** — a cape specific to Flag Tag
- **Unlock cost:** 300 coins + 20 wins
- **Must be equipped** to be eligible for MANA rewards
- Visible in-world — acts as a status symbol and free advertising

## Updated Boomerang Pricing
| Item | Coins | Wins Required |
|------|-------|---------------|
| Yellow Boomerang | 50 | 1 |
| Green Boomerang | 150 | 5 |
| Blue Boomerang | 200 (was 300) | 10 |
| **Cape** | **300** | **20** |

## MANA Reward System
- **5 MANA** per round win
- Player must have cape **equipped** (not just owned)
- **Minimum 3 players** in the round to qualify
- **No daily cap** — competition is the natural limiter
- Wallet auto-dispenses to winner

## Anti-Bot Measures
- Cape gate requires significant gameplay investment (300 coins + 20 wins = ~3-5 hours)
- Equipped wearable check — bots can't just hold it in a wallet
- Minimum player count prevents solo farming
- Competition itself limits wins per player

## Launch Week Promo
- **Duration:** 1 week (or until budget runs out, whichever first)
- **Budget:** ~$500 USD (~7,000 MANA)
- That's ~1,400 possible wins, ~200 per day
- Consider announcing cape availability a week before promo so players can grind toward eligibility
- Optionally display a live "prize pool remaining" counter for urgency

## Post-Launch Strategy
Based on launch week results, consider:
- Monthly MANA weekends
- Seasonal tournaments with bigger prizes
- Making it permanent if the numbers work
- Starting limited and expanding is easier than walking back a permanent system

## Coin Economy Summary
Coins are earned at ~5-25 per round (base + pickups). Current sinks:
- Boomerang upgrades (gameplay advantage)
- Music tracks (personalization) — coming soon
- Cape (status + MANA eligibility)
